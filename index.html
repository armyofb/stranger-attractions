About

Stranger Attractions Presents brings the heaviest touring acts and the best of the local underground to Indianapolis — 780+ shows and counting. Death metal, doom, thrash, punk, grind: if it's loud, we book it.

You'll find us most nights at Black Circle and State Street Pub. Almost every show is all ages. Come early, support the openers, buy the merch.
